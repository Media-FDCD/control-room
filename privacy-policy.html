Privacy Policy

Franklin Media Control Room - last updated September 27, 2026

The Franklin Media Control Room is an internal publishing tool operated by Franklin Dental Care (Franklin, Ohio) to schedule and post the practice's own videos to its own social media accounts, including TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

What we collect

When a member of our media team connects one of our social accounts, the platform gives the tool an access token and basic account information (account id, display name, avatar). The tool stores these tokens in Google Apps Script script properties, visible only to the Google Workspace account that owns the tool.

How we use it

Tokens are used solely to upload and publish videos, captions and thumbnails that our team has prepared, and to read basic publishing status for those posts. We do not read, collect or store data about viewers, followers or other users of the platforms.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to the social platform being posted to and to Google, where the tool and its media files are hosted.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected from the tool or the token expires. Disconnecting an account deletes its token. To request deletion of any data, email media@franklindental.care.
